## 📧 Sample 1 – Best Intern Award Scam

The first email attempts to deceive the recipient by claiming they have won the **DecodeLabs Best Intern Award**, including a **₦10,000,000 cash prize**, free visa processing to Canada, and other attractive rewards.

### Phishing indicators identified:

* Suspicious sender address
* Fake website link
* Unrealistic rewards
* Sense of urgency
* Requests for sensitive information
* Grammar and spelling mistakes

---

## 📧 Sample 2 – Fake DecodeLabs Portal Verification

The second email impersonates the **DecodeLabs Security Team** and attempts to steal internship portal credentials by claiming the recipient's account will be suspended unless it is verified immediately.

### Phishing indicators identified:

* Fake sender domain
* Credential harvesting attempt
* Suspicious verification link
* Fear and urgency
* Requests for login credentials
* Grammar and spelling mistakes
